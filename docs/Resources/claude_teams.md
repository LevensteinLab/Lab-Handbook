# Claude Team Access

The lab has a Claude Teams subscription. Claude is an AI assistant that can help with writing, coding, data analysis, literature review, and more.

To join the lab's team plan, use this invite link: [Request Access to Claude Teams](https://claude.ai/join/org#IupnwohhQUWa82Yi6PmC2g). You'll need to sign up for a Claude account (or link your existing one) and your request will be approved by the team admin. The above link expires 10/29/2026. If the link has expired, ask Dan for a new one and update this page.

## Usage limits

Standard seats come with a usage limit. When you hit it, your account should switch over to the lab's usage credits so you can keep working (credits are capped per person per month). If it doesn't switch, and you get a message that you've hit your limit and Claude won't accept prompts, let Dan know and he'll fix it.

If you find yourself hitting the limit regularly, ask Dan to upgrade you to a premium seat. That's usually cheaper for the lab than running through credits every month, and you shouldn't ration a tool that's helping your research. Premium seats also come with access to other models (with their own, separate quotas), so if you hit a dead end with one, try another.

For guidelines on using generative AI responsibly and effectively in research, see the [GenAI](https://levensteinlab.github.io/Lab-Handbook/Resources/gen_ai/) page.
